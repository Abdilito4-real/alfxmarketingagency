Alfredo’s journey began in 2022, rising from grassroots community engagement to mastering on-chain trading and market psychology. In 2025, Alfredo officially founded Alf X, transforming a global network of specialists into a premier growth agency. Built on the core principle of high-impact coordination, the agency was designed to move beyond fragmented tactics and deliver sustained visibility for crypto-native brands.

Today, Alf X serves as a leading visibility partner across major ecosystems like Solana, Ethereum, and Base. By combining strategic positioning with cross-regional distribution, we help projects cut through market noise and scale authority in highly competitive landscapes.
